COMPOUND-HETEROZYGOUS AGGREGATION
=================================

Scope
-----
This is a gene-and-disease-level evidence stage for recessive small-variant
candidates. It is independent of any patient, gene, disease, coordinate, or
validation truth row.

Phase policy
------------
confirmed_trans
    Two heterozygous variants have opposite alternate haplotypes and share
    the same PS or PID phase block.

likely_cis
    Two heterozygous variants have the alternate allele on the same
    haplotype in a shared PS or PID phase block.

possible_compound_heterozygous
    Multiple heterozygous variants are present but the VCF does not prove
    trans configuration. This requires segregation or phasing review.

single_recessive_allele
    Only one qualifying heterozygous variant is present for the same
    gene-disease relationship.

homozygous_biallelic
    A homozygous alternate genotype already fulfils the biallelic genotype
    requirement. No compound score is added.

Scoring policy
--------------
The stage updates the existing inheritance component; it does not add a new
independent score dimension.

confirmed_trans                 target inheritance points = 3
possible_compound_heterozygous  target inheritance points = 1
likely_cis                      no additional credit
single_recessive_allele         no additional credit
homozygous_biallelic            existing full credit retained

Unphased variants are never labelled confirmed trans.

Preserved VCF FORMAT fields
---------------------------
GT, PS, PID, PGT, DP, GQ, and AD are preserved in the extracted table where
present. The current validation VCFs have no PS, PID, PGT, or phased GT, so
their heterozygous pairs can only be classified as possible, not confirmed.
