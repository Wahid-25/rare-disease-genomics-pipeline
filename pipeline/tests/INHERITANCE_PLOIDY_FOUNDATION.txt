UNIVERSAL INHERITANCE AND PLOIDY FOUNDATION
===========================================
Inheritance precedence:
1. mitochondrial
2. X-linked G2P forms
3. autosomal recessive/biallelic
4. autosomal dominant/monoallelic
5. unknown

Sex metadata:
- production default: unknown
- explicit --sex male|female|unknown supported
- validation mode may resolve sex from sample_sheet.csv

Ploidy preflight:
- GRCh38 X/Y PAR1 and PAR2 aware
- autosomal, X, Y, PAR and MT calls reported
- biological inconsistencies warn by default
- structural VCF errors fail

Compound heterozygosity:
- not inferred by this foundation
- requires a separate gene-level phase-aware aggregation stage
